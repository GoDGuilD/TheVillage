# Known issues

- Neighbouring areas each keep their own ring of trees, so the border between two areas is a four-tile tree belt;
  it reads as forest but is wider than ALttP's borders.
- Ledges only face south (ALttP also has east/west ledges); add them when a level needs one.
- The villagers' lines follow the draft story (docs/GDD.md section 2), which the owner has not confirmed; the
  title subtitle "A STORY BEFORE THE CHAPEL" is a placeholder.
- Bombs cannot be lifted and thrown yet (ALttP can); arrows also cut bushes (ALttP's do not).
- The boomerang only stuns; in ALttP it also defeats a few weak enemies.
- Torches go out when the area is left, so both meadow torches must be lit on one visit.
- After the item-get pose Joe faces down (as in ALttP), so a second purchase needs a tap toward the counter.
- One save slot only (ALttP had three); text is English only until Phase 14.
- Enemies and cut bushes come back every time an area is re-entered (ALttP does the same for most screens).
- The leaf burst reads as small rings; nicer leaves in the polish pass.
- No music; the 37 sound effects have never been auditioned by ear.
- The 2024 audio in `assets/audio/` has unverified licences.
- Nothing has been playtested by the owner yet (feel numbers are first guesses).
- "Moss Shrine", its rooms and the Grand Slime are first drafts (names and layout await the owner); the shrine
  facade is plain and gets a pass in the polish phase.
- Push blocks reset when their room is rebuilt (ALttP does the same); the heavy switch stays pressed once pressed.
- Enemies cannot fall into pits (they treat them as walls); a thrown pot or bomb over a pit does not fall either.
- Only one dungeon exists, so the overworld map only shows areas already visited and has no markers for dungeons.
- A dungeon death continues at the current room's entry (ALttP sends you to the dungeon entrance).
- All dungeons share the Moss Shrine's stone tiles and entrance facade; Ram's Hollow, the Frost Vault and the keep
  get their own palettes in the polish phase. The keep's wall reads as a flat dark block from above.
- The end of Act 1 returns to the title: the Dark World (Phase 8) will continue from the save outside the keep.
- Caver's shade, the Iron Ram and the Frost Wraith are first drafts (patterns, HP, names await the owner).
- The dash cannot cross pits or ledges and does not knock items off trees (ALttP's boots do).

- The Dark World has no interiors or shops yet, and its houses are shut ruins; the four dimension regions are signs
  only until Phases 9-12. A mirror landing on a Light World bush or pot overlaps it (Joe can walk out).
- Rifts and portals are flat swirls y-sorted with the actors: Joe standing just above one is drawn under it.
- The grapple only shoots in Joe's four facings, and a pull never crosses into another area (posts are placed
  inside one screen). Joe is not invincible while being pulled.
- Dark rooms forget their lit torches when Joe leaves (torches are not saved), so a dark room is dark again on
  return. The darkness is a soft circle, not ALttP's lantern cone.
- The Gloom Moth's grounded frame is the flying frame with folded wings, close to the floor; its dust is not
  blockable by the shield.
- The Shade Market reuses the indoor table tiles outdoors.
