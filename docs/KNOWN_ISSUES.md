# Known issues

- Neighbouring areas each keep their own ring of trees, so the border between two areas is a four-tile tree belt;
  it reads as forest but is wider than ALttP's borders.
- Ledges only face south (ALttP also has east/west ledges); add them when a level needs one.
- The villagers' lines follow the draft story (docs/GDD.md section 2), which the owner has not confirmed; the
  title subtitle "A STORY BEFORE THE CHAPEL" is a placeholder.
- Boni's shop has no stock yet, and the item menu has no items to show until Phase 5 (slots read "-").
- The magic meter has nothing to spend magic on until the first magic item.
- One save slot only (ALttP had three); text is English only until Phase 14.
- Enemies and cut bushes come back every time an area is re-entered (ALttP does the same for most screens).
- The leaf burst reads as small rings; nicer leaves in the polish pass.
- No music; the 25 sound effects have never been auditioned by ear.
- The 2024 audio in `assets/audio/` has unverified licences.
- Nothing has been playtested by the owner yet (feel numbers are first guesses).
